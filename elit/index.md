# elit

Experimental literature and interactive fiction projects that explore computational creativity, constraint-based writing, and digital storytelling.

## Interactive Fiction

### Like a Mountain of Sleep
An experimental interactive fiction piece built with SugarCube. A meditation on memory, place, and the weight of dreams.

### What you and I know about you
A surveillance poetry widget that transforms browser tracking data into live, accumulating verse. The piece operates entirely client-side—no data is transmitted—revealing the usually invisible mechanics of web surveillance through experimental literature.

## About elit

elit encompasses projects that push the boundaries of traditional narrative forms through:

- **Interactive Fiction**: Twine/SugarCube-based narrative experiences
- **Constraint-based Writing**: Works exploring OuLiPo techniques and generative methods
- **Digital Storytelling**: Experimental approaches to narrative in digital spaces
- **Computational Creativity**: Algorithmic and procedural text generation

This section documents experimental literary works that exist at the intersection of technology and creative writing, exploring how computational tools can expand the possibilities of textual expression.
