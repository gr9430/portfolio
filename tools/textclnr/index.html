Cleaning is never neutral. Every rule throws something away, and what counts as noise depends on what you plan to do next. A word-frequency count and a close reading want different texts. Turn rules off when they take something you need.

The Gutenberg licence has a machine-readable marker. A book's own title page and table of contents do not — they are part of the edition, so no rule can find them with certainty. Rule 04 makes its best guess, tells you exactly where it cut, and lets you say otherwise.
