The first spoke of language (langue)-speech (parole), the second of schema-usage. We can also add competenceperformance in Chomsky's language.

> [!PDF|red] [[Ricoeur - The Model of the Text - Meaningful Action Considered as a Text.pdf#page=3&selection=57,0,59,47&color=red|p.3]]
> > First trait: Discourse is always realized temporally and in a present, whereas the language system is virtual and outside of time. Emile Benvtniste calls this the "instance of discourse."

> [!PDF|red] [[Ricoeur - The Model of the Text - Meaningful Action Considered as a Text.pdf#page=3&selection=61,0,74,74&color=red|p.3]]
> > Second trait: Whereas language! lacks a subject-in the sense that the question "Who is speaking?'does not apply at its level-discourse refers to its speaker by means of a complex set of indicators such as the personal pronouns. We will say that the "instance of discourse" is self-referential

> [!PDF|red] [[Ricoeur - The Model of the Text - Meaningful Action Considered as a Text.pdf#page=3&selection=76,0,89,13&color=red|p.3]]
> > Third trait: Whereas the signs in language refer only to other signs within the same system, and whereas language therefore lacks a world just as it lacks temporality and subjectivity, discourse is always about something. > [!PDF|red] [[Ricoeur - The Model of the Text - Meaningful Action Considered as a Text.pdf#page=3&selection=91,0,104,48&color=red|p.3]]
> > Fourth trait: Whereas language is only the condition for communication, for which it provides the codes, it is in discourse that all messages are exchanged. In this sense, discourse alone has not only a world, but an other-another person, an interlocutor to whom it is addressed.
