# Interdisciplinary Teaching

Graduate coursework in the theory and practice of designing interdisciplinary courses and curricula for the humanities. This work addresses foundational questions of pedagogy while exploring the challenges and opportunities of generative AI tools in contemporary humanities education.

## Context

**Interdisciplinary Teaching** (Spring 2026)
*College of Arts and Humanities, University of Central Florida*

This graduate-level course bridges theoretical engagement with pedagogical literature and practical application in course design. As both PhD coursework in Texts and Technology and professional development for academic career preparation, the work explores how to create meaningful connections across disciplinary boundaries while maintaining rigorous scholarship and inclusive teaching practices.

## Course Components

**[Discussion Posts](/teaching/interdisciplinary-teaching/discussion-posts/)** — Weekly reflective responses to readings and pedagogical questions, exploring how theory translates to classroom practice.

**[Signature Assignment](/teaching/interdisciplinary-teaching/signature-assignment/)** — Major project demonstrating mastery of interdisciplinary course design principles, integrating pedagogical theory with practical application.

**[Course Syllabus](/teaching/interdisciplinary-teaching/syllabus/)** — Development of a complete syllabus for an interdisciplinary humanities course, incorporating course readings, feedback, and best practices.

**[Teaching Statement](/teaching/interdisciplinary-teaching/teaching-statement/)** — Specialized statement focusing on interdisciplinary approaches in humanities education, distinct from composition-focused teaching philosophy.

**[Final Portfolio](/teaching/interdisciplinary-teaching/final-portfolio/)** — Comprehensive portfolio bringing together all semester work with reflective analysis of pedagogical development.

## Theoretical Framework

This coursework engages with contemporary questions in humanities pedagogy:

- How do students learn most effectively across disciplinary boundaries?
- What assignment and assessment strategies support interdisciplinary thinking?
- How can courses be designed to be both inclusive and intellectually rigorous?
- What role should generative AI tools play in humanities education?
- How do we balance theoretical engagement with practical skill development?

The work contributes to broader questions in Texts and Technology regarding the changing landscape of humanities education in digital contexts.
