# Published Interviews

Produced and published video interviews with writers working outside academic institutions, made for pedagogical use in first-year composition courses (ENC 1101 and ENC 1102) at UCF. Each interview models non-academic writing practices, creative process, and the relationship between form and audience — subjects central to composition instruction. These recordings also serve as examples of multimodal public scholarship: interviews produced, edited, and published as standalone research outputs.

---

## Interview with Christopher Norris (Steak Mtn)

Christopher Norris is a visual artist whose work under the name Steak Mtn spans album art, illustration, and graphic work across underground music communities. His practice engages questions of image, labor, and the visual culture of hardcore and punk.

## Interview with John Trefry (Inside the Castle)

John Trefry is the publisher of Inside the Castle, an independent press devoted to formal and experimental literature. This interview addresses the editorial and design commitments of the press, the relationship between literary form and physical book-making, and what it means to publish outside conventional academic and commercial channels.
