# CV of Failures
Entering a PhD program at 31 from an 'alt music'/DIY background means constantly navigating the gap between institutional validation and the work itself, especially as someone who aims to nurture a decolonial position in their work. This document exists to normalize rejection as part of my development and remind myself that persistence often matters more than any single outcome.

    -  Waitlisted for admission; received late admission without funding.

## Conference Rejections
