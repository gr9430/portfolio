# <span class="animated-header">Lebor I</span>
*Self-published as Lebor Publications, limited pamphlet run, June 2023*

Limited-edition pamphlet featuring experimental writing and object design, published to incite new ideas and retire old ones. Described as "an unmixed, unmastered, non-existing dance mix single with a bloated preamble," it blends conceptual critique with farcical reflection.

Written, edited, and designed by Glenn S. Ritchey III, with implementation of the custom typeface Amalgam by [Benjamin Tuttle](https://btuttle.com/type.html). Managed all aspects of production, from content to exclusive distribution at [Topos Bookstore](https://www.toposbookstore.com/about), Ridgewood, Queens, NY.
