For about five years, I contributed my graphic art to one pocket of the DIY arts scene in Orlando and Daytona Beach, FL--not only as a graphic artist, but as a musician and booker for the music scene I was a member of. I also served as head film programmer (2019-2021) at Cinematique Theater in Daytona Beach, where the screenings and the flyers advertising them were both mine to make .Elsewhere--at Spectacle Theater in Brooklyn, NY, for instance--I volunteered design work for programming that was someone else's 

Flyers, tour posters, and screening promos for bands I played in (Witchbender, Ad Nauseum, Deformed, Flying Limbs) alongside dozens more I had no stake in beyond proximity and admiration. The work was never separable from the practice it documented: a show needed a flyer the way it needed a PA, and whoever could make one, did. That collapsing roles--performer, promoter, archivist, all the same, largely unpaid, person--helped animate the question I now ask through expanded toolsets: how do we democratize meaning-making? It's one I still answer by hand when a project calls for it.
