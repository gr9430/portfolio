
## Professionalism (20% of Final Grade)
### Purpose
Part of being successful in college and in your future career is learning how to navigate the many responsibilities we carry in our day-to-day lives. This professionalism assignment is designed to help you develop habits of accountability, communication, and professional conduct that will benefit you indefinitely—in your academic work, future careers, and personal life.

### Components & Grading Breakdown
- Total Points Available: 125
    - Attendance: 100 points
    - Professional Communication: 25 points

#### Attendance (100 points)
All students begin the semester with 100 points for attendance. Regular presence in the classroom—both showing up and meaningfully engaging with the course—is essential to your learning and to our classroom community.

##### The 3-Day Policy
You have 3 absences that require no explanation or documentation.

I have enacted this policy because I know there are many reasons that might conflict with your ability to attend class: illness, family emergencies, mental health needs, personal matters, or other unexpected conflicts. I want to be mindful of students who might not have regular access to healthcare or who might have unconventional needs to miss class.

- You do not need to provide a doctor's note or explanation for these 3 absences.

- You must email me if you are going to miss class.

- I expect at minimum 3 hours' notice if you know you'll miss class, but 12 hours is preferred.

- For emergencies, email me as soon as you are able.

- You cannot make up in-class work for these absences, as our in-class activities depend on contributing to the classroom community in real time
Use these days sparingly—they're your buffer for the entire semester

##### University-Sanctioned Absences
Absences for official university business (athletics, academic competitions, university-sponsored club activities, etc.) do not count toward your 3 absences, but you must provide documentation. Please submit this paperwork as early as possible so we can plan accordingly.

##### Absence Penalties Beyond 3

- 4th absence: -15 points
- 5th absence: -30 points (total)
- 6th absence: -50 points (total)
- 7+ absences: Attendance grade may reach 0

##### Tardiness
- Arriving late is better than missing class entirely, but punctuality is a professional expectation. Canvas will automatically track tardiness, which may affect your attendance grade. Chronic tardiness may be addressed individually.

- Always anticipate that it will be hard to find parking and get to campus an hour before class, if possible.

### Professional Communication (25 points)
At the end of the semester, I will assess your professional communication holistically based on our interactions throughout the term. Everyone begins with full credit (25 points). Deductions will only occur for consistent patterns of unprofessional behavior, not isolated mistakes. I will consider the following:

#### Email professionalism

- Appropriate greetings, clear subject lines, respectful tone, proper sign-offs.

- Proactive communication: Notifying me in advance of absences, asking questions when confused, reaching out about conflicts early rather than last-minute.

- Responsiveness: Replying to my emails in a timely manner when a response is needed.

- Overall professional demeanor: Taking responsibility, following through on commitments, treating our course interactions as professional practice.

This is not about perfection—we're all learning. I'm looking for consistent effort to communicate professionally and handle your responsibilities maturely. If you're unsure whether your communication style meets expectations, please ask!

#### Email
Please be polite in our interactions. I know these formalities might feel extra, but practicing them now will serve you well. Many of your future professors, supervisors, and colleagues will expect this level of professionalism.

- Opening your emails:

    - "Dear Professor Ritchey"
    - "Hi Professor Ritchey"
    - "Good morning/afternoon/evening Professor Ritchey"

Or something similar in nature

- Closing your emails by signing off with appropriate conventions such as:

    - "All best," or "Best,"
    - "Warmly,"
    - "Sincerely,"
    - "Thanks," 
    - "Thank you,"

#### Example format:

    Dear Professor Ritchey,

    [Your message here]

    All best,
    [Your name]
