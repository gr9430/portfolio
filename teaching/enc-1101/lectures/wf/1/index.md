## What This Course is About

- Recognizing the writing you already do
    
- Learning how language and power connect
    
- Building a community of writers
    
- _Not about grammar rules or five-paragraph essays_
    
### 3 Units, 4 Major Assignments

1. Multimodal Literacy Narrative
    
2. Textual Analysis of a Literacy Practice
    
3. Revise & Remediate (one of the above)
    
4. ePortfolio + Presentation (about your semester's work)
 
## Class Policies

### How You'll Be Graded

- **60%** Major Assignments
    
- **20%** Professionalism (attendance + communication)
    
- **15%** Participation (readings + activities)
    
- **10%** Drafting & Peer Review
    
### Participation

Three main components throughout the semester:

- **Perusall:** Weekly reading engagement
    
- **Reading Summaries:** 8 of your choice
    
- **Discussion Posts:** A few strategic ones
    

### About Perusall

- Online annotation platform for our readings
    
- You interact with the text AND each other
    
- Auto-graded based on quality of engagement
    
- _Think of it as collaborative note-taking_
    

### Why Perusall?

- Helps you prepare for class discussions
    
- Builds reading comprehension actively
    
- Creates a conversation around the texts
    
- _You're not just reading—you're thinking with the material_

### Reading Summaries

- Complete **8 summaries** over the semester
    
- Choose any 8 weeks from Weeks 1-15
    
- Template provided for structure and consistency
    
- _Build your knowledge base strategically_
    
- Each summary includes:

    - Citation, argument, key points

    - Key terms, quotations, strengths/limitations

    - Connections to student learning outcomes

    - Connections to other authors we've read
    
- Not busy work—this builds your ePortfolio
    
- Helps you connect concepts across the semester
    
- Prepares you for ENC 1102 literature reviews
    
- _You're creating your own reference library_
    
### ePortfolio Check-Ins

- Periodic progress checks throughout the semester
    
- Upload screenshots/links showing portfolio development
    
- Brief reflections on your progress
    
- _Prevents end-of-semester scrambling_
    
### Attendance

- You get **3 absences**, no questions asked
    
- Must email me as soon as you know
    
- Beyond 3 = affects your Professionalism grade
    
- _Use them wisely and remember we all signed up to be here, so let's respect the classroom environment._
    
### Late Work

- In most cases, late work will not be accepted

- _The late policy exists for genuine emergencies_
