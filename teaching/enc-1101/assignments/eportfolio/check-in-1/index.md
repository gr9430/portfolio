# ePortfolio Check-In #1: Foundation & Setup

## Purpose

This check-in ensures you've established your Google Sites ePortfolio and begun documenting your learning. Since ePortfolios are semester-long projects, starting early prevents last-minute scrambling.

### Requirements (Complete all items for full credit)

#### Platform & Structure
(Submit link via Canvas)

- Created ePortfolio using Google Sites (free version)

- Set up basic navigation structure (Home, Learning Outcomes, Major Assignments, Writing Artifacts, etc.)

- CRITICAL: Changed share settings to "Anyone with the link can view"

- Tested that link works by opening it in an incognito/private browser window.

#### Content
(Must be visible on your site)

- Include at least ONE learning outcome with your initial understanding explained in 3-4 sentences

-  Include at least TWO writing artifacts from Weeks 1-5 (examples: Academic Engagement Activity, Reading Summaries 1-2, in-class work, discussion responses) with brief reflection (2-3 sentences each) on why you chose them

- Your MLN Outline or early draft work uploaded/embedded with brief context about where you are in the process

#### Reflection Component

Write 1 short paragraph (4-6 sentences) addressing:

- What has surprised you most about your writing or language use so far this semester?

- Which learning outcome feels most relevant to your work right now, and why?

#### Submission

Submit your Google Sites link through Canvas. I will check that all required elements are present and accessible. This is completion-based grading.

##### Common Google Sites mistake
Students forget to change share settings. If I can't access your site, you won't receive credit until it's fixed.
