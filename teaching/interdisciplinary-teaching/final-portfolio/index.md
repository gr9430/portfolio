# Final Portfolio and Reflection

The culminating project for the course, this portfolio brings together all semester work and includes a comprehensive reflection on growth in understanding interdisciplinary pedagogy.

## Portfolio Components

The final portfolio will include:

- **Discussion Posts Collection** — All weekly reflective responses with meta-commentary
- **Signature Assignment** — Complete interdisciplinary course design with rationale
- **Syllabus** — Final version of developed course syllabus
- **Teaching Statement** — Refined statement on interdisciplinary teaching philosophy
- **Reflective Essay** — Comprehensive reflection on learning and pedagogical development

## Reflection Focus Areas

The reflective component will address:

- Evolution of understanding about interdisciplinary teaching
- Integration of course readings and theoretical frameworks
- Practical applications for future teaching contexts
- Lessons learned about inclusive and student-centered pedagogy
- Approaches to AI integration in humanities education
